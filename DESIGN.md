# Hawking — Industrial Hazard Signage

The black hole is the hazard; the site is the facility built around it. Every surface is a sign
someone bolted to a wall: square corners, thick black borders, no shadows, no gradients except
the hazard tape itself.

## Color meaning (ISO 3864)
- **Yellow on black**: warning. Headlines, the masthead tape, the top of every slab.
- **Red**: prohibition and loss. The red zone (No. 1-5), selling, spaghettified wallets.
- **Blue**: mandatory action. Containment fund (treasury), "hold 100,000".
- **Green**: the safe condition. The top ten's share.
- **Amber**: the amber zone (No. 6-10).

## Components
- **Masthead**: black bar with a 6px hazard tape along the bottom; the active nav item is a solid yellow block.
- **Placard** (hero): white sign, black "Warning" band, the holder at the core, a 24-segment Geiger level.
- **Slab**: every section sits on a dark slab with a 12px hazard tape top edge.
- **Core plate / zones**: the exposure board, yellow plate for No. 1, red and amber zone headers.
- **Posts**: the cycle's four steps as screwed-on white placards, sized by fee share; the running one has a blinking beacon.
- **Rule signs**: four ISO signs (mandatory, warning, safe, prohibition).
- **Caution tape bands** between sections, two tapes crossing, scrubbed by scroll.
- **Close**: a yellow field with a black-and-yellow chevron edge, "You have been warned."

## 3D
Same light black hole as Horizon (shader disk, halo, photon ring, orbiting bodies with names),
held in a hazard-striped torus on steel struts with rotating red/amber beacons. 30 fps cap, paused
when hidden, dpr drops on slow devices.

## Rules
No rounded corners, no drop shadows, no em/en dashes in visible text, no yield/APY language.
